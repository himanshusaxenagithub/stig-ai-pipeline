Essays

Ten notes on public checklists and everyday computers

Himanshu Saxena · written to be published on stig.hsaxena.com or hsaxena.com

These pages are for a reader who may never open a terminal. They explain what a STIG is, why a school or clinic might care, and how this open-source tool keeps a person in charge of every check.

DISA — the Defense Information Systems Agency — publishes Security Technical Implementation Guides for the Department of Defense. The guides are publicly released. This project does not speak for DISA. It tries to make those public baselines readable and checkable on a desk Mac or Windows PC.

  • 2. What a STIG is, without the jargon

    A lockdown checklist with ranked severity — not a secret document, not a virus scanner, not a DoD endorsement of this website.

  • 3. When the school or clinic has no security team

    The same commercial operating systems, the same lost-laptop risk, and no specialist to translate a 200-rule guide.

    Schools · clinics · small offices

  • 4. Settings gaps are not Hollywood hacking

    Most of a STIG is configuration: encryption off, automatic login on, unused services left running.

    Hygiene · myths
